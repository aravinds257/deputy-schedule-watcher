Type your Deputy email, password, and organization subdomain into the Email & Password tab and click Sign In. The app will authenticate directly with Deputy and fetch your token automatically.

If you are already logged in to https://a2c28219075424.uk.deputy.com on another tab:

  2. Click any API request (e.g. shifts or api).
  3. Under Request Headers, copy the Bearer token string after authorization: Bearer and paste it in the Bearer Token tab.
